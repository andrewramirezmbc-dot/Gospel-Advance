    The Mission

    [01 / Our mission]

    Reaching college students with the gospel of Jesus Christ.

    Gospel Advance exists to bring the hope of Jesus to the next generation. We meet students on campus, engage their questions in honest conversation, and invite them to follow Christ. Then we help them connect with existing Christian ministries on their campus so they can grow in faith and community.

    The Gospel

    The message that changes everything.

    Not a self-help program. Not advice. An announcement: God has done for you what you could never do for yourself.

    01

    God Created

    We were made in God’s image, designed for relationship with Him and to reflect His glory.

    Genesis 1:27
    02

    We Rebelled

    Every person has sinned and fallen short. Our rebellion separated us from a holy God.

    Romans 3:23
    03

    Christ Redeemed

    Jesus lived perfectly, died for our sin, and rose again victorious over death.

    Romans 5:8
    04

    We Respond

    Repent and believe. Christianity doesn’t start with a feeling. It starts with an empty tomb.

    Acts 17:30–31
    For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish but have eternal life.
    — John 3:16
