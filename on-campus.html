    Why college
    campuses?

    College is a formative season. Students are considering identity, purpose, faith, and the future. We meet them in that season with a willingness to listen and a clear invitation to consider Jesus.

    Our mission is not limited to college students. Through biblical media, resources, and training, we also equip believers to share Christ in their churches, homes, and communities.

    Our approach

    Three simple steps.
    A lasting impact.

    We meet students where they are, with genuine conversation, clear truth, and ongoing connection.

    01

    Start a conversation.

    We ask about faith and life, listen to students' questions, and engage respectfully. Participation in a conversation or filmed interview is voluntary.

    02

    Make Christ known.

    We explain the gospel, open Scripture, and invite students to consider who Jesus is. The goal is a genuine conversation, not winning an argument.

    03

    Keep growing.

    We help interested students connect with Christian ministries on their campus for continued discipleship and community.

    A closer look

    What could a visit
    look like?

    A visit may include student conversations, time with a campus ministry, or an evangelism workshop. Together we can discuss your setting, campus requirements, scheduling, and follow-up.
