    Questions about
    the mission.

    Ask us a question
    What happens during a campus visit?

    We engage students in honest conversations about faith and life, share the gospel, and invite them to consider who Jesus is. To discuss a visit to your campus, get in touch.

    What happens after a conversation?

    We help interested students connect with Christian ministries on their campus so they can grow in faith and community.

    What does financial support help fund?

    Support helps fund campus outreach, gospel conversations, travel, and the work of reaching the next generation with Jesus Christ.

    Are gifts tax-deductible?

    Gospel Advance is not currently a registered nonprofit, and we do not represent gifts as tax-deductible charitable contributions. Please consult your tax adviser about your own circumstances.

    How can I manage monthly support?

    To request a change or cancellation, contact us with the email address used for your gift. Please do not send card numbers or other payment credentials.

    Christ Redeemed

    Jesus lived perfectly, died for our sin, and rose again victorious over death.

    Romans 5:8
