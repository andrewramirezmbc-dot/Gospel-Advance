    Our mission

    A broad mission.
    A strategic starting point.

    We strategically engage college students through personal conversations, campus outreach, biblical media, and training. Campuses bring us into contact with young adults who are asking important questions about life and faith.

    The work extends beyond campus. Our films, messages, and free Academy courses help believers grow in Scripture and become better equipped to share Christ wherever they live.

    01

    Who We Are

    Gospel Advance is a Christian evangelistic ministry reaching the next generation and equipping believers to make Christ known. College campuses are a strategic starting point. We exist to make the gospel known in a clear, winsome, and biblical way.

    02

    What We Do

    We engage students where they are: on campuses, online, and in their everyday lives through real conversations, articles, videos, and training. Our content is designed to equip believers and spark meaningful conversations about life, faith, and Jesus.

    03

    Why It Matters

    This generation is searching for truth, purpose, and hope. We believe the gospel is the power of God for salvation (Romans 1:16). Our mission is to see lives transformed by the good news of Jesus Christ and to help others do the same.

    Jesus calls His followers to make disciples and teach them to obey Him. That calling shapes our message and our purpose.

    Matthew 28:19–20

    Our founder

    Making Christ known.

    My desire is to help people know the hope of Jesus Christ and to equip believers to share that hope with others. Gospel Advance brings these efforts together through conversations, preaching, media, and training with a focus on the next generation.

    This is about more than content. It is about people. Real conversations. Real questions. And a real gospel that still changes lives today.
