    About Gospel Advance

    Reaching the next generation through gospel conversations.

    We exist to reach the next generation with the gospel and equip believers to make Christ known.

    A broad mission.
    A strategic starting point.

    We strategically engage college students through personal conversations, campus outreach, biblical media, and training. Campuses bring us into contact with young adults who are asking important questions about life and faith.

    The work extends beyond campus. Our films, messages, and free Academy courses help believers grow in Scripture and become better equipped to share Christ wherever they live.

    Jesus calls His followers to make disciples and teach them to obey Him. That calling shapes our message and our purpose. Matthew 28:19–20

    Andrew Ramirez / Evangelist

    Making Christ known.

    Andrew shares the gospel through personal conversations, preaching, and biblical teaching. Gospel Advance brings these efforts together with a focus on the next generation.

    His desire is to help people know the hope of Jesus Christ and to equip believers to share that hope with others.
